# Eisenstein Hermitian packet determinant

**Author:** Francesco Giannicola  
**Truth status:** new intermediate theorem; no rank-30 curve is claimed.

## Orbit data

Let `sigma` generate the cyclic cubic deck group. Choose orbit
representatives

\[
P_1,\ldots,P_n
\]

on the cubic genus-one pullback. For each pair put

\[
h_{ij}^{(k)}=
\langle P_i,\sigma^kP_j\rangle,
\qquad k=0,1,2.
\]

Let `zeta` be a primitive cube root of unity and define

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal H_{ij}
=h_{ij}^{(0)}+\zeta h_{ij}^{(1)}+\zeta^2h_{ij}^{(2)}.
}
\]

Galois invariance implies that `mathcal H` is a Hermitian matrix over the
Eisenstein integers `Z[zeta]`.

For every genus-one cyclic trisection orbit,

\[
h_{ii}^{(0)}=6+2m_i,
\qquad
h_{ii}^{(1)}=h_{ii}^{(2)}=2m_i,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{\mathcal H_{ii}=6.}
\]

Thus every orbit has Hermitian norm six, independently of its intersection
with the zero section.

## Trace and projection

Let

\[
T_i=P_i+\sigma P_i+\sigma^2P_i
\]

and let `v_i in E8` be its trace vector. Then

\[
\langle T_i,T_j\rangle=3\langle v_i,v_j\rangle_{E8}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
h_{ij}^{(0)}+h_{ij}^{(1)}+h_{ij}^{(2)}
=\langle v_i,v_j\rangle_{E8}.
}
\]

The projected representatives

\[
q_i=P_i-\frac{T_i}{3}
\]

satisfy

\[
\langle q_i,\sigma^kq_j\rangle
=h_{ij}^{(k)}-\frac{\langle v_i,v_j\rangle}{3}.
\]

The trace residues are mutually orthogonal modulo three exactly when these
projected pairings are integral.

## Difference lattice

The `Z`-lattice generated by the differences

\[
P_i-\sigma P_i,
\qquad
\sigma P_i-\sigma^2P_i
\]

is the trace lattice of the Hermitian Eisenstein lattice with Gram matrix
`mathcal H`. Since `disc(Q(zeta))=-3`, its determinant is

\[
\boxed{
\det L_{\mathrm{diff}}
=3^n\operatorname{Norm}_{\mathbf Q(\zeta)/\mathbf Q}
(\det\mathcal H).
}
\]

For one orbit, `mathcal H=[6]` and the trace form is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}12&-6\\-6&12\end{pmatrix}=A_2(6),
\]

whose determinant is `108=3*Norm(6)`.

## Projected lattice

Multiplication by `1-zeta` sends the projected orbit lattice to the difference
lattice. Its Eisenstein norm is three, so the index is `3^n`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\det L_{\mathrm{proj}}
=3^{-n}\operatorname{Norm}(\det\mathcal H).
}
\]

For one orbit this is `36/3=12`, the determinant of `A2(2)`.

The invariant lattice is `E8(3)`, of determinant `3^8`. Hence the orthogonal
sum of the invariant lattice and an `n`-orbit projected packet has determinant

\[
\boxed{
\det L_{\mathrm{packet}}
=3^{8-n}\operatorname{Norm}(\det\mathcal H).
}
\]

## Rank-thirty consequence

For the required `n=11` nontrivial character directions,

\[
\boxed{
\det L_{30}
=\frac{\operatorname{Norm}(\det\mathcal H)}{27}.
}
\]

Thus a putative rank-thirty packet must satisfy all of the following:

1. `mathcal H` has Hermitian rank eleven;
2. every diagonal entry is six;
3. the trace residues satisfy the exact mod-three integrality constraints;
4. `Norm(det mathcal H)` is divisible by 27;
5. the resulting determinant is compatible with the integral Neron--Severi
   discriminant and with saturation.

This gives a fast exact independence and regulator filter. A nonzero
Hermitian determinant proves independence of the twenty-two trace-zero
rational directions over `Q`, once arithmetic descent of the orbit sections
has been established.

## Search implementation

For each common cyclic cover, record the three integer height correlations
`h^(0),h^(1),h^(2)` for every pair of orbit representatives. Build
`mathcal H` over `Z[zeta]`, compute its exact determinant and norm, and reject
the packet before specialization unless the determinant and divisibility
checks pass. This avoids assembling a full 30 by 30 real Gram matrix at every
intermediate step.

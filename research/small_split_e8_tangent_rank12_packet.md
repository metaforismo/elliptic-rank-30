
## Exact lower bound

The direct plane-cubic verifier reduces the points modulo good primes, forms
`E(F_p)/5E(F_p)`, and obtains a combined row rank of 12. A good-reduction
prime whose group order is prime to 5 excludes rational 5-torsion. Infinite
descent therefore proves that the selected twelve points

```text
B0 B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 B6 B7 D1_0 D2_0 D1_1 D2_1
```

are `Z`-independent modulo torsion.

A second implementation in SageMath 10.9 maps the plane cubic to a
Weierstrass model and independently obtains the same rank-12 certificate.

Consequently, the subgroup generated by the fourteen displayed points has
**exact rank 12**.

# Research notes

Each note states its scope and truth status.  Executable certificates live next
to the argument or under `certificates/`; tests are under `tests/`.

Current themes:

- Galois-character rank packets and their branch-genus constraints;
- exact obstructions to overly symmetric or torsion-heavy packet designs;
- Mordell--Weil lattice visibility and hidden Galois orbits;
- modular reinterpretations of isotrivial `j=0` searches;
- Riemann--Roch norm constructions with enough visible points to leave room
  for rank 30;
- failure analysis that changes the representation rather than merely raising
  a search bound.

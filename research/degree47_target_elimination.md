# Exact elimination of the generic degree-(4,7) target system

**Status:** `proved exact symbolic computation`.

## Normalization

Assume the squarefree/coprime Mason equality case and write

\[
Q(v)^2-v^2L(v)^3=v^3-Sv^2+3v+1. \tag{1}
\]

Replace \(Q\) by \(-Q\) if necessary, so

\[
Q(0)=1,\qquad [v]Q=\frac32.
\]

The Wronskian equality is

\[
2LQ+3vL'Q-2vLQ'=\lambda. \tag{2}
\]

Its constant term is \(2L(0)\). The coefficient of \(v\) forces the linear
coefficient of \(L\) to vanish. Put

\[
L=A(1+bv^2+cv^3+dv^4),\qquad A\ne0. \tag{3}
\]

Equation (2) recursively determines all coefficients \(q_2,\ldots,q_7\) of
\(Q\) as rational functions of \(b,c,d\). The coefficient of \(v^3\) in (1)
then gives

\[
\boxed{c=\frac2{11}-3b.} \tag{4}
\]

The coefficients of degrees \(8,9,10\) in (2) leave three exact equations in
only \(b,d\). After clearing denominators and primitive contents, these are

\[
1936d^2-6534db+396d+99b^2-6b=0, \tag{5}
\]

\[
4356d^2+363db^2-154db-1617b^3-891b^2+126b-4=0, \tag{6}
\]

and

\[
85184d^2b^2-395307db^2+50094db-1584d
+484605b^3-88176b^2+5346b-108=0. \tag{7}
\]

## Exact Gröbner result

The lexicographic Gröbner basis of (5)--(7) over \(\mathbf Q\), with variable
order \(d>b\), is

\[
\boxed{[1]}. \tag{8}
\]

Thus the ideal generated by the reduced equations is the unit ideal:

\[
\langle E_8,E_9,E_{10}\rangle=\mathbf Q[b,d].
\]

Consequently there is **no common zero even over an algebraic closure of
\(\mathbf Q\)**. In particular, there is no rational pair \((b,d)\), so the
leading cube-class test and final reconstruction stage are vacuous.

This is stronger than merely finding an elimination polynomial without
rational roots. The earlier post-processing expected a nonconstant univariate
polynomial and failed when SymPy correctly returned the constant basis element
\(1\); the corrected verifier now treats the unit ideal as the strongest
possible nonexistence certificate.

## Coverage

For a squarefree target cubic, any common zero of \(Q\) and \(vL\) would give a
square or cube factor in the cubic remainder, so the supports are disjoint.
Mason equality then forces squarefreeness and the Wronskian identity (2).
Therefore every generic rational degree-(4,7) target identity is represented
by (5)--(7) and is excluded by (8).

The repeated-root values \(S=-3\) and \(S=15/4\) are covered independently by
`certificates/degree47_degenerate_obstruction.json`.

## Independent cross-check

The separate standard-library certificate
`degree47_target_mod19_obstruction.py` derives two linear equations in a
rescaled variable and shows that their primitive determinant quintic has no
root modulo \(19\). Thus the generic branch is excluded by two mathematically
different exact computations:

1. a unit-ideal Gröbner basis over \(\mathbf Q\);
2. a primitive rational-root obstruction modulo \(19\).

## Interpretation

Together with the separate repeated-root obstruction, this closes every
polynomial degree-(4,7) target identity. Combined with the degree-(2,4)
classification and the Mason--Stothers degree bound, it closes the entire
polynomial representation at the historical coefficient \(3v\).

It does not cover rational functions with finite poles and does not solve the
global rank-30 challenge.

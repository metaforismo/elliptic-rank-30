## Exact advances in this branch

1. **Symmetric packet obstruction.**  A split genus-zero `V4` packet over a
   real field cannot admit a lifted order-three symmetry cycling its three
   nontrivial characters; the would-be conic is the nonsplit form
   `u^2+v^2=-1`.  Faithful genus-one `V4` actions also have a distinguished
   fixed-point-free involution, so the three characters cannot be cyclically
   permuted there either.
2. **Modular `j=0` reduction.**  The cyclic sextic family is the quadratic
   pullback of a rational elliptic surface along `s=t(t-1)`.  Its first missing
   anti-invariant section reduces to the CM curve `Y^2=X^3-432`; the proved
   Sage computation is designed to decide whether this branch contains any
   non-torsion rational parameter.
3. **Torsion capacity obstruction.**  Rational two-torsion forces root-lattice
   cost at least four on a rational elliptic surface, so a smooth three-branch
   packet has total generic rank at most 22.  Rational three-torsion lowers the
   bound to 14.  Easy split descent is therefore the wrong high-rank target.
4. **Riemann--Roch visibility barrier.**  `N` rational zeros of one function
   with pole divisor `N O` satisfy one principal-divisor relation.  In
   particular, a degree-30 split near-square can span rank at most 29.  The
   minimal one-function target compatible with rank 30 is the 31-zero norm
   equation

       A(x)^2 - R(x) B(x)^2 = c product_{i=1}^{31}(x-r_i),
       deg A <= 15, deg B = 14, deg R = 3.

5. **Split `E8` construction track.**  General-position cubic pencils through
   eight rational points are searched until a square-free degree-12
   discriminant certifies twelve `I1` fibres and split generic rank eight.
   The resulting rational surface is then scanned for high-rank quadratic
   packets.

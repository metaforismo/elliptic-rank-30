# Roadmap

## Gate A — maintain the certified rank-29 baseline

Keep exact equation/point verification, heights, local data, saturation,
hashes, and independent Sage/Magma checks reproducible.

## Gate B — force more generic directions

1. Certify a split `E8` rational elliptic surface with twelve `I1` fibres.
2. Compute all three quadratic packet channels for branch triples.
3. Promote only explicit characteristic-zero sections.
4. Target twist ranks summing to at least 22.

## Gate C — independent low-height construction

Solve the 31-zero norm equation

```text
A(x)^2 - R(x) B(x)^2 = c * product_{i=1}^{31}(x-r_i)
```

and prove that 30 of the 31 visible points are independent.

## Gate D — record certification

Once 30 points exist: exact equation checks, torsion, local independence,
canonical-height matrix, determinant, saturation analysis, adversarial audit,
second-CAS verification, machine-readable package, and paper update.

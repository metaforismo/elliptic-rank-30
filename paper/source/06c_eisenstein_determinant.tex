\section{Exact Eisenstein packet determinants}

For orbit representatives $P_1,\ldots,P_n$, put
\[
h_{ij}^{(k)}=\langle P_i,\sigma^kP_j\rangle,
\qquad k=0,1,2,
\]
and define the Eisenstein Hermitian matrix
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal H_{ij}
=h_{ij}^{(0)}+\zeta_3h_{ij}^{(1)}+\zeta_3^2h_{ij}^{(2)}.
}
\]
Galois invariance makes $\mathcal H$ Hermitian over
$\mathbf Z[\zeta_3]$.  Every diagonal entry equals six, independently of the
intersection of the trisection with the zero section.

Let $T_i=P_i+\sigma P_i+\sigma^2P_i$ have $E_8$ trace vector $v_i$.  Then
\[
h_{ij}^{(0)}+h_{ij}^{(1)}+h_{ij}^{(2)}
=\langle v_i,v_j\rangle_{E_8}.
\]
The projected pairings are integral precisely when the trace residues obey
the mod-three orthogonality constraints.

The difference lattice generated by
\[
P_i-\sigma P_i,
\qquad
\sigma P_i-\sigma^2P_i
\]
is the trace form of the Hermitian lattice $\mathcal H$.  Since
$|\disc\mathbf Q(\zeta_3)|=3$,
\[
\boxed{
\det L_{\rm diff}=3^n\operatorname{Norm}(\det\mathcal H).
}
\]
Multiplication by $1-\zeta_3$ maps the projected lattice onto the difference
lattice with index $3^n$.  Therefore
\[
\boxed{
\det L_{\rm proj}=3^{-n}\operatorname{Norm}(\det\mathcal H).
}
\]
Including the invariant $E_8(3)$ lattice gives
\[
\boxed{
\det L_{\rm packet}=3^{8-n}\operatorname{Norm}(\det\mathcal H).
}
\]
For the rank-thirty target $n=11$,
\[
\boxed{
\det L_{30}=\frac{\operatorname{Norm}(\det\mathcal H)}{27}.
}
\]

This formula is an exact early filter.  A putative eleven-orbit packet must
have nonzero Hermitian determinant, satisfy the ternary integrality
conditions, have norm divisible by twenty-seven, and yield a determinant
compatible with the integral N\'eron--Severi lattice and saturation.  The full
thirty-by-thirty real Gram matrix is needed only after these cheaper exact
checks pass.

\begin{abstract}
We study the constructive problem of exhibiting an elliptic curve over
$\Q$ with at least thirty independent rational points.  The objective is not
an impressive analytic-rank score but a final, independently checkable lower-
rank certificate: one nonsingular Weierstrass equation, thirty complete
rational points, exact equation checks, a rigorous independence proof, a
canonical-height matrix, saturation data, provenance, hashes, and a second
computer-algebra verification.

The current public rank-$29$ record is reproduced as the baseline.  We then
separate several obstructions that are often conflated: generic lattice
capacity, arithmetic descent, visibility of low-height points, auxiliary-base
genus, specialization dependence, and certification cost.  Exact results
include a branch-genus obstruction for multiquadratic packets, a principal-
divisor obstruction to one-function thirty-point constructions, capacity
bounds for torsion-friendly rational elliptic surfaces, and rigidity of
elliptic fibrations when $\chi(\mathcal O_S)\ne2$.

The principal new setting is a cyclic cubic genus-one pullback of a split
$E_8$ rational elliptic surface.  A degree-three elliptic base is the first
low-genus base change capable of geometric Mordell--Weil rank thirty.  We
classify genus-one trisection classes by shells of $E_8$, prove that every
cyclic trisection orbit projects to an $A_2(2)$ plane independently of its
intersection with the zero section, and identify an exact ternary trace code
in $E_8/3E_8$.  The minimal norm-six shell contains $6720$ classes, but an
integral projected packet is confined to one of $2240$ maximal totally
isotropic four-spaces, each containing only $240$ minimal classes.  This
reduces the decisive search to a finite incidence problem: find eleven
Hermitian-independent cyclic trisection orbits defining the same positive-
rank genus-one cover.  No rank-$30$ curve is claimed in this draft.
\end{abstract}

\paragraph{Truth status.}
The baseline proves $\rank E(\Q)\ge29$ for the published record curve.  The
new results are intermediate theorems and exact search reductions.  They do
not prove that rank $30$ exists, and they do not prove a universal upper bound
of $29$.

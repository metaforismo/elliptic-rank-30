\section{Hessian reduction of the cyclic locus}

Let $X=\{F=0\}\subset\mathbf P^3$ be the marked cubic surface attached to a
minimal trace class, and let $\ell$ be the 3-secant line through the three
contracted section points.  A hyperplane $H$ not containing $\ell$ determines
\[
q=H\cap\ell,
\qquad
C_H=X\cap H,
\]
and the trisection map is projection of the plane cubic $C_H$ from $q$.

\subsection{The invariant polar form}

Write $F(v)=T(v,v,v)$ for the symmetric trilinear polarization.  For
$q\notin X$, put
\[
a=F(q),
\qquad
\lambda_q(u)=T(q,q,u),
\]
and define
\[
\boxed{
B_q(u,v)
=aT(q,u,v)-\lambda_q(u)\lambda_q(v).
}
\]
This form descends to the three-dimensional quotient $V/\langle q\rangle$.
If $H=\langle q,W\rangle$, then in coordinates on $H$ with
$q=(1:0:0)$ the restricted cubic is
\[
F_H=ax^3+x^2L(y,z)+xQ(y,z)+C(y,z).
\]
The substitution
\[
x=X-\frac{L}{3a}
\]
removes the $X^2$ term.  Projection is cyclic precisely when the remaining
mixed quadratic vanishes:
\[
\boxed{3aQ-L^2=0.}
\]
In invariant form,
\[
\boxed{B_q|_W=0.}
\]

\subsection{The Hessian criterion}

In a basis beginning with $q$, write
\[
M_q=
\begin{pmatrix}
a&\lambda^T\\
\lambda&Q_q
\end{pmatrix}
\]
for the matrix of $T(q,-,-)$.  The matrix of $B_q$ on the quotient is
$aQ_q-\lambda\lambda^T$.  The block determinant formula gives
\[
\boxed{
\det B_q=a^2\det M_q.
}
\]
Since the Hessian matrix of $F$ at $q$ is $6M_q$,
\[
\boxed{
\det B_q=0
\iff q\in\operatorname{Hess}(X).
}
\]
Over an algebraic closure, a symmetric form on a three-dimensional space has
a two-dimensional totally isotropic subspace exactly when it is singular.
Therefore a geometrically cyclic hyperplane section is equivalent to a point
$q\in\ell\cap\operatorname{Hess}(X)$, outside $X$, together with a maximal
isotropic plane of $B_q$.

\subsection{A finite quartic search}

The Hessian of a cubic surface is quartic.  If $\ell$ is not contained in it,
there are at most four possible projection centres.  At a generic rank-two
Hessian point, the form $B_q$ has exactly two maximal isotropic planes over the
algebraic closure.  Thus a generic trace class has at most
\[
\boxed{8}
\]
geometrically cyclic hyperplane sections.

For a maximal ternary trace code with $240$ minimal classes, the continuous
hyperplane search collapses to at most
\[
\boxed{240\cdot8=1920}
\]
explicit candidates.  Each is then tested for rational descent, smoothness,
avoidance of the elliptic discriminant, and positive rational rank.

This is the current decisive representation.  The search is no longer an
unstructured square-discriminant condition in a projective three-space.  It is
one Hessian quartic restricted to one 3-secant line, followed by the solution
of a rank-two quadratic form.

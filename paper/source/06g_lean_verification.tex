\section{Lean verification of the exact algebraic layer}

The repository contains a machine-checked Lean 4 formalization, pinned to
Lean and mathlib version 4.32.2.  The root module is
\texttt{EllipticRank30.lean}; continuous integration builds it and rejects
local \texttt{sorry}, \texttt{admit}, and \texttt{axiom} declarations.

The Lean development certifies the following exact ingredients used in this
paper:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item the birational identity taking
  $u^3=c\,t(t-1)$ to $Y^2=X^3+16c^4$;
\item positivity of $\mu^2-\mu+1$ over the reals and the resulting
  impossibility of the minimal real constant equation;
\item the generic marked-coefficient factorization and the symmetric product
  and pairwise-product identities for its three rational roots;
\item the repeated-factor and quadratic-twist identities at the degenerate
  marking $\mu=2$;
\item both normalized rational solutions used in the minimal-scale
  obstruction;
\item every numerical Euler, root-rank, rank-capacity, shell-size, and
  trace-code count used in the six-channel reduction.
\end{itemize}

This formalization has a deliberately narrow truth boundary.  It does not yet
formalize Kodaira classification, minimal elliptic surfaces, Shioda--Tate,
Picard-number bounds, character decomposition of Mordell--Weil groups,
function-field rank computations, canonical heights, saturation, or rational
point independence.  Those statements remain supported by the exact proofs
and independent SageMath/Magma certificates described elsewhere in the
paper.  Thus a green Lean build strengthens the algebraic core but is not, by
itself, a rank-thirty certificate.

\section{The six-channel capacity criterion}

The degeneration at $\mu=2$ does not occur for a generic rational marking.
Let
\[
a=\mu^2-\mu+1,
\qquad
b=\frac{a^3}{\mu(\mu-1)},
\qquad
P_\mu(v)=b^2v^2-a^3(v+1)^3.
\]
Exact factorization gives
\[
P_\mu(v)=
-\frac{a^3}{\mu^2(\mu-1)^2}
\bigl(v-\mu(\mu-1)\bigr)
\bigl(\mu^2v+\mu-1\bigr)
\bigl((\mu-1)^2v-\mu\bigr).
\]
Thus the three roots are rational. They are distinct and avoid $0$ and
$-1/4$ outside
\[
\mu\in\left\{0,1,\frac12,2,-1\right\}.
\]

Over an algebraic closure, the full cyclic cubic genus-one base is obtained
by adjoining $u^3=cv$ and $\sqrt{1+4v}$. The $C_3\times C_2$ character
decomposition consists of the six sextic-twist channels
\[
E_{i,\varepsilon}:\quad
 y^2=x^3+v^{2i}(1+4v)^{3\varepsilon}P_\mu(v),
\qquad i=0,1,2,
\quad\varepsilon=0,1.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Six-channel capacity]
Away from the five degenerate markings, the geometric Mordell--Weil rank
capacities of the six channels are
\[
\boxed{(4,6,4,4,6,6)}.
\]
Their sum is exactly thirty. The three rational elliptic surface channels
have exact geometric ranks $4,6,4$. Consequently the full packet has
geometric rank thirty if and only if the three K3 channels have Picard number
twenty, equivalently if they attain ranks
\[
\boxed{4,6,6}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The roots of $P_\mu$, $0$, $-1/4$, and infinity are distinct. Applying the
Kodaira table to the exponents of
$v^{2i}(1+4v)^{3\varepsilon}P_\mu(v)$ gives the following root ranks:
\[
4,\ 2,\ 14,\ 4,\ 12,\ 12.
\]
The corresponding arithmetic genera are
\[
1,\ 1,\ 2,\ 1,\ 2,\ 2.
\]
Since $h^{1,1}=10\chi$ over $\mathbf P^1$, Shioda--Tate gives the capacity
vector $(4,6,4,4,6,6)$. Rational elliptic surfaces have Picard number ten,
so the three $\chi=1$ bounds are attained automatically. The remaining
sixteen directions are attained precisely when each K3 surface has Picard
number twenty.
\end{proof}

This is the first exact reduction of the rank-thirty target to three finite
Picard-maximality tests. The scale $c$ affects rational descent and the rank
of the genus-one base but not the geometric capacity vector.

\subsection{Finite-field rejection sieve}

For each rational marking, the three K3 channels are reduced at several good
primes and their exact finite-function-field geometric ranks are computed.
Any rank below $(4,6,6)$ rejects the marking. A survivor is not a proof of
characteristic-zero rank: it is promoted only after explicit sections and an
independent height certificate are constructed.

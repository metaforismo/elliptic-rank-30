\section{Truth-status classification and remaining uncertainty}

\subsection{What is proved}

The repository proves the following statements unconditionally.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item The published record curve contains twenty-nine independent rational points.
\item Thirteen independent quadratic splitting characters cannot be carried by a low-genus multiquadratic base; the minimum genus is $20481$.
\item A single quadratic character can carry more than one independent Mordell--Weil direction.
\item The zeros of one rational function satisfy one forced group-law relation; a thirty-zero one-function construction spans rank at most twenty-nine.
\item Torsion-friendly rational surfaces with forced reducible fibres have insufficient packet capacity for rank thirty.
\item Elliptic fibrations with $\chi(\mathcal O_S)\ne2$ are canonically rigid; the K3 case is exceptional.
\item The displayed rational elliptic surface has split Mordell--Weil lattice $E_8$ and twelve $I_1$ fibres.
\item A degree-three genus-one pullback is the first degree-three low-genus mechanism whose Hodge capacity reaches rank thirty.
\item Genus-one trisection classes are indexed by $E_8$ shells of norm $6(m+1)$.
\item Every cyclic trisection orbit projects to $A_2(2)$, independently of $m=D\cdot O$.
\item The minimal trace-code incidence geometry consists of $2240$ maximal isotropic four-spaces, each containing $240$ norm-six trisection classes.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{What is not proved}

No curve with thirty certified independent rational points has been produced.
No cyclic trace code has yet been shown to contain eleven trisection orbits
defining one positive-rank cover.  Picard maximality and arithmetic descent of
the proposed cubic pullback are unresolved.  The degree-$31$ norm identity has
not been solved over $\Q$.  The project also has no universal theorem bounding
$\rank E(\Q)$ by twenty-nine.

\subsection{Restricted impossibility statements}

The obstructions in this paper are mechanism-specific:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item arithmetic genus $d\le3$ over $\mathbf P^1$ cannot give generic rank thirty;
\item four independent quadratic characters cannot be carried by a rational or elliptic multiquadratic base;
\item a thirty-zero one-function construction has a forced relation;
\item ramification over an $I_1$ fibre prevents a cubic genus-one pullback from attaining the Hodge ceiling thirty;
\item torsion-heavy rational surfaces cannot supply the required packet capacity.
\end{itemize}
None is a universal upper bound for elliptic curves over $\Q$.

\subsection{Adversarial audit for a future Level-5 candidate}

A putative rank-$30$ result must survive the following independent checks:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
\item nonsingular global minimal model;
\item thirty complete exact rational points satisfying the equation;
\item no duplicate, negative, or torsion generators;
\item rigorous canonical-height matrix and positive determinant;
\item exact independence by finite reduction or descent;
\item saturation at every necessary prime;
\item agreement of two computer algebra systems;
\item hashes, software versions, and search provenance;
\item proof that no floating-point coordinate or cached transformation entered the certificate;
\item comparison against known curves and isomorphism canonicalization.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Current final status}

The most accurate classification of this draft is
\[
\boxed{\textbf{new intermediate theorem}.}
\]
The cyclic cubic trace-code representation is a finite and falsifiable search
space aligned with a final proof certificate.  It is not yet a candidate rank-
$30$ construction.

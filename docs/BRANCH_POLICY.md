# Branch policy

`main` is the only persistent research branch.

Maintainer work may be prepared in a local Git worktree, but the remote state is consolidated into `main` after verification. Temporary remote branches must be deleted or reset to `main` immediately after their pull request is merged or superseded.

External contributors may use short-lived pull-request branches. Those branches are deleted after merge. No mathematical claim is considered part of the project until it is present on `main` with its certificate and truth-status label.

# Mathematical claim checklist

Before changing the truth status, verify:

- the object is defined over the claimed field;
- every displayed point or section is exact;
- independence is not inferred from point count;
- Selmer and analytic information are not used as lower bounds;
- specialization and coordinate transformations are recorded;
- numerical matrices have rigorous or independent exact support;
- all conditional assumptions are named;
- failed counterexample searches are documented;
- the final wording matches the strongest proved category and no stronger.

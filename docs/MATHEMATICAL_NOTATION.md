# Mathematical notation

- `E/Q` denotes an elliptic curve over the rationals.
- `K=Q(t)` denotes a rational function field.
- `rank` without qualification means algebraic Mordell--Weil rank.
- `r0` is the base generic rank; `r1,r2,r3` are quadratic character ranks in a
  `V4` packet.
- `O` is the identity section or point.
- Characteristic-p computations are labelled with their base field and are not
  silently transported to characteristic zero.

# Finite-field discovery policy

Finite-field rank computations may identify promising fibre configurations,
branch packets, or norm identities.  They do not establish a rank statement
over `Q`.

Promotion requires:

- an explicit characteristic-zero lift;
- exact rational sections or points;
- proof that the lifted sections specialize from the claimed family;
- an exact independence certificate;
- rejection of supersingular or accidental reduction artifacts.

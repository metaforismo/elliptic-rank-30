# Failure analysis

Every substantial route must record:

## A long and useful failed route

Why the mechanism looked capable of producing thirty directions, its exact
equations, what was computed, and the smallest decisive obstruction.

## What the failure reveals

Which lattice, branch, descent, height, or Galois structure survived.

## The decisive change of setting

The exact new representation dictated by the obstruction.  A larger search
bound without a changed mathematical model is not a decisive change.

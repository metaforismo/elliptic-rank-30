# Candidate escalation levels

- Level 0: arithmetic score only.
- Level 1: at least 25 verified independent rational points.
- Level 2: at least 28 verified independent rational points.
- Level 3: 29 independent points plus a distinguished additional point or
  unresolved covering.
- Level 4: 30 putative points, not yet rigorously certified.
- Level 5: 30 rigorously independent rational points.

Only Level 5 is a record.
